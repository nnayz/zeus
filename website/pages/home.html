Questions

Is Taskmaster free?
Yes. Taskmaster is free and open source under Apache 2.0. You only pay for the agents you use, through your own Claude, ChatGPT or other accounts.
Which agents does it work with?
Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, OpenCode, Pi, Copilot, Kimi and more: 22 agent definitions ship with the app, and any terminal agent can be added with a short JSON file.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You describe tasks in plain English and the agents do the work. The guides are written for marketers and product managers as well as developers.
What happens when I close Taskmaster?
Your agents keep working. Reopen Taskmaster and every session is where you left it. Restarting your Mac stops them, but Claude Code, Codex and most others can resume the same conversation.
Can agents work together?
Yes. Claude Code, Codex and Cursor sessions started in Taskmaster can start other agents, hand them tasks, and collect their results. Ask in plain English, starting with “Use Taskmaster to…”.
Does Taskmaster see my code or prompts?
No. Agents run on your Mac or your own servers, with no Taskmaster account and no hosted relay. Nothing is uploaded to Taskmaster.
Which platforms are supported?
macOS 15 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel, from one universal build.
